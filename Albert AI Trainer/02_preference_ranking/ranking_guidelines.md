# AI Response Preference Ranking Guidelines

## Goal
Choose the better of two responses for a specific prompt and explain the choice.

## Decision criteria
- Accuracy and sound reasoning
- Relevance to the prompt
- Instruction following, including exact format or length
- Clarity and audience fit
- Completeness without unnecessary detail
- Safety when relevant

## Process
1. Read the prompt carefully.
2. Evaluate A and B against the same criteria.
3. Select the better response; do not choose based on length or confident tone alone.
4. Write a specific justification.
5. Note when the choice is close or depends on the user's intended priority.

## Practice note
This is a synthetic practice set. Some examples can support nuanced discussion; reviewers should explain their reasoning and avoid pretending every preference decision is perfectly objective.
